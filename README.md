# Superstore-Sales-Analysis
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Superstore sales performance across regions, customer segments, product categories, and states with business insights and data-driven recommendations.

## Project Overview

This project presents a complete end-to-end sales analytics workflow using Python and Power BI on the Superstore dataset.

The analysis began with Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) in Python to understand data quality, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and sales patterns. The insights derived from EDA were then transformed into an interactive Power BI dashboard to support business decision-making.

The objective of the project is to identify key revenue drivers, evaluate customer and product performance, analyze geographic trends, and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Project Workflow

Data Collection → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Data Analysis (Python) → Business Insights → Interactive Dashboard Development (Power BI)

## Key Insights

- Sales increased from $479.9K in 2015 to $722.1K in 2018, demonstrating strong business growth.
- The West Region generated the highest revenue among all regions.
- Technology emerged as the highest revenue-generating category.
- Consumer customers contributed more than half of total sales.
- California and New York were the strongest performing states.
- Revenue was concentrated among a small group of high-value customers.

## Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in Technology products.
- Expand successful strategies from the West Region to underperforming regions.
- Strengthen customer retention programs for high-value customers.
- Improve market penetration in lower-performing regions.
- Prepare inventory planning around peak sales periods.
